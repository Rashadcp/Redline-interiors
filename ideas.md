# Chosen Direction: Gallery in Motion

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial modernism** informed by architecture monographs, boutique hospitality lookbooks, and the spare precision of Swiss-style art direction. The site should have a rhythm of full-bleed rooms and rigorous margin systems rather than a conventional marketing-page sequence.

## Core Principles

1. **Rooms lead; interfaces recede.** Photography carries the emotional weight while controls remain slim, purposeful, and spatially aware.
2. **Asymmetry creates poise.** Content aligns to shifting editorial axes, with measured offsets and overhangs instead of repeated centered modules.
3. **Red is an incision, not a field.** The signature red appears as a line, marker, index, and moment of action.
4. **Silence is premium.** Generous breathing room, quiet surfaces, and short, considered copy make each project feel deliberate.

## Color Philosophy

The identity is built on **paper-white**, **bone**, and **near-black**, which behave like a gallery environment: neutral enough to let natural stone, timber, and light in the imagery retain their richness. **Architectural red (#C52A22)** is used sparingly as a directional mark—simultaneously a drafter’s annotation and a confident editorial underline. It should always feel intentional rather than decorative.

## Layout Paradigm

The page follows an **architectural promenade**: a cinematic entry hall, an offset editorial essay, a two-part studio narrative, a photographic project sequence, then a measured service ledger and invitation. The dominant desktop grid is a 12-column field, but text and image blocks deliberately break it through left-aligned rails, edge-to-edge frames, and visual overlaps. Mobile resolves this into a single continuous vertical walk without losing the scale shifts.

## Signature Elements

1. **The red datum line:** a 1–2px vermilion rule that progresses, expands, or marks a section threshold.
2. **Project index numerals:** fine, oversized numbers used as visual anchors beside photography.
3. **Cropped serif statements:** large editorial headings that reach beyond their nominal column and use italic emphasis only for material words.

## Interaction Philosophy

Movement is quiet and physical. The header gains a solid paper surface as the visitor enters the content. Image hovers feel like a lens slowly moving closer; section links leave behind a red trace. Navigation is explicit and accessible, never hidden behind theatrical interaction.

## Animation

The hero image settles from a modest 1.08 scale with a 1.6-second cinematic ease; text reveals through clipped vertical masks in a 70ms word stagger. Scroll-linked movement is limited to fractional image translation and the progress of red rules. Project hover reveals use a 350ms opacity and transform sequence, and the mobile experience disables nonessential motion under reduced-motion preferences. No bouncing, parallax excess, spinning graphics, or gratuitous scroll-jacking.

## Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** is the display voice: high-contrast, architectural, and elegantly reserved at large scale. **Manrope** handles navigation, metadata, service lists, and body copy with technical clarity. Display headings are uppercase with restrained tracking, while micro-labels use Manrope in uppercase at 0.68rem with broad letter spacing. Never use Inter.

## Brand Essence

**Redline Interiors composes enduring residential and commercial environments for clients who value spatial intelligence as much as atmosphere.**

**Personality:** Exacting, cultivated, quietly assured.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are declarative and compressed. CTAs sound like a considered invitation, not a conversion funnel. Microcopy uses plain, precise language and avoids buzzwords.

Examples: “A measured response to the way you live.”

“Begin with the space you have in mind.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **redline monogram**: two parallel red architectural strokes turned at right angles to suggest an open room plan. The logo pairs this symbol with a compact, bespoke-looking REDLINE wordmark in black, stacked over a tracked INTERIORS descriptor. The symbol must remain legible as a favicon and distinctly separate from the wordmark.

## Signature Brand Color

**Redline Vermilion — #C52A22.**

## Style Decisions

- The red datum line forms one connected editorial system across section openings, project indices, navigation cues and primary actions; it is never generic decoration.
- Project numerals act as oversized anchors that give the photographic sequence a clear art-book rhythm.
- CTA and section copy use compressed, spatial language and avoid generic marketing phrasing.
